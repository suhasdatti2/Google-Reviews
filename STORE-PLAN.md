## 5. FAQ section (already built into the theme homepage)

**How does the card work?**
There's an NFC chip inside the card, programmed with your Google review link. When a customer holds their phone near the card, their phone reads the chip and opens your Google review page — the same technology as tap-to-pay.

**Do my customers need an app?**
No. NFC reading is built into modern iPhones and Android phones. Customers just unlock their phone and hold it near the card — no app, no QR code, no typing.

**How do you program my review link?**
At checkout you'll enter your business name and Google review link. We program the chip, tap-test it on both iPhone and Android, and ship it ready to use. Nothing to set up on your end.

**What if I don't know my Google review link?**
No problem. Order anyway and email us at sales@optiqservice.com — we'll send you a 2-minute guide to finding it, or find it for you if you share your business name and city.

**Are there any monthly fees?**
No. It's a one-time purchase. You own the card, the link is programmed directly onto the chip, and there's nothing to renew or subscribe to.

**Which phones does it work with?**
Virtually all iPhones and Android phones from the last several years support tap-to-read NFC out of the box. If a customer has a much older phone, they can still find you on Google the usual way.

**Does it guarantee 5-star reviews?**
No — and nothing legitimately can. What the card does is remove the friction: instead of asking customers to search for your business and find the review button, one tap takes them straight there. What they write is entirely up to them.

**Can the card be reprogrammed if my link changes?**
Yes. If your Google review link ever changes, email us and we'll walk you through reprogramming it or sort out a replacement.

## 7. Shipping & processing policy (Settings → Policies → Shipping policy)

**Processing time**
Every card is programmed with your Google review link and individually tap-tested before it ships. Please allow **1–3 business days** for programming and quality checks. If we have a question about your review link, we'll contact you at the email or phone number provided at checkout — responding quickly keeps your order on schedule.

**Shipping time**
- Standard shipping (US): 3–7 business days after processing
- You'll receive a confirmation email with tracking as soon as your order ships

**Didn't include your review link at checkout?**
Email it to sales@optiqservice.com with your order number. Processing starts once we have your link.

**Wrong or missing package?**
If your order hasn't arrived within 10 business days of shipping, or arrives damaged, email sales@optiqservice.com and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.

## 8. Refund policy (Settings → Policies → Refund policy)

We want your card working on your counter, not sitting in a drawer.

**Defective or not working**
If your card arrives damaged or doesn't open your review page when tapped, contact us within **30 days of delivery** at sales@optiqservice.com. We'll troubleshoot with you, and if the card is faulty we'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund — your choice.

**Wrong link programmed**
If we programmed a different link than the one you provided, we'll replace the card free of charge, no questions asked.

**Changed your mind**
Because each card is custom-programmed for your business, we can't restock opened, programmed cards. Unopened cards can be returned within 30 days for a refund of the card price (buyer pays return shipping).

**What we can't refund**
Cards that work correctly but haven't produced the review volume you hoped for. The card makes leaving a review easier — it can't make customers write one, and we're honest about that before you buy.

To start a return or replacement, email sales@optiqservice.com with your order number.

## Launch checklist (do these in Shopify admin after importing the theme)

1. **Import theme:** Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload zip → `optiqservice-theme.zip` → Publish
2. **Create the product:**
   - Title: `Google Review NFC Card` (handle will be `google-review-nfc-card` — the theme's buttons link to it)
   - Paste the product description from section 3
   - Add a variant option named "Pack": `1 Card` — $19.99, `2 Cards` — $34.99, `3 Cards` — $49.99
   - Upload product photos when ready (theme shows a card mockup until then)
3. **Create pages:**
   - Page "About" → paste section 6 copy → theme template `page.about`
   - Page "Contact" → theme template `page.contact` (copy is built in)
4. **Menus (Online Store → Navigation):**
   - Main menu: How it works (`/#how-it-works`), Pricing (`/#pricing`), FAQ (`/#faq`), About (`/pages/about`), Contact (`/pages/contact`)
   - Footer menu: About, Contact, or leave default (theme has sensible fallbacks)
5. **Policies (Settings → Policies):** paste sections 7 and 8; add a standard privacy policy from Shopify's generator
6. **Checkout personalization:** already handled — the product page asks for Business name, Google review link, and Email/phone as required fields; they appear on every order automatically. The page also tells buyers they can email the link to sales@optiqservice.com instead.
7. **Sender email:** Settings → Notifications → set sender to sales@optiqservice.com
8. **Test order:** place one, confirm the three personalization fields show up on the order, then refund it
9. Launch. Improve photos and copy after it's live — done beats perfect.

**Compliance note baked into all copy:** we never claim guaranteed 5-star reviews — the card "makes leaving a review easier." The footer also carries a "not affiliated with Google" line. Keep both.
